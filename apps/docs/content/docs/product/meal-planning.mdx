---
title: Meal planning
description: Turn a recipe collection into a practical weekly plan.
---

The meal plan schedules recipes by date and meal type. Each planned meal can override
servings without changing the underlying recipe.

Supported meal types are:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner
- Snack

Use the weekly view to spot gaps, rebalance repeated meals, and prepare a shopping
list before the week begins.
