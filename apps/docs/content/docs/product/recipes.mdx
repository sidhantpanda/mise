Recipes are the durable center of Mise. Each recipe contains familiar cooking fields
while retaining its Schema.org representation.

## Add a recipe

Use **Recipes → New recipe** to enter a recipe manually. Name and ingredients are the
most important fields; preparation time, yield, nutrition, images, sections, and
instructions can be added as available.

To bring structured recipes from another system, upload or paste Schema.org Recipe
JSON-LD. Mise also supports an optional public library catalog configured through
`PUBLIC_LIBRARY_URL`.

## Recipe instructions

Instructions can be simple ordered steps or named sections containing steps. This
maps to Schema.org `HowToStep` and `HowToSection`, so multi-part recipes do not need
to be flattened.

## Portability

The household settings include an export of the recipe library. Keep this export
with regular database backups when recipes are especially important.
